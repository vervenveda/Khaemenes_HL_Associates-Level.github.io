KHL-CORE-106 · Module 09 · Lesson 01

Medication Labels and Safe Use

Follow labels and professional instructions; avoid sharing or improvising medication use.

Safety boundary

This lesson is educational only. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe. Urgent symptoms, emergencies, self-harm risk, severe distress, poisoning, overdose, serious injury or other immediate danger require qualified emergency or medical support.

Concept frame

How to reduce harm around alcohol, drugs and medicines without prescribing.

Practice

Explain follow labels and professional instructions; avoid sharing or improvising medication use. in non-diagnostic, educational language.
Identify one reliable health-information source for this topic and why it is appropriate.
Name one warning sign or situation where self-help should give way to qualified professional support.
Describe one low-risk, practical habit or preparation step connected to this lesson.
State one harmful misconception or overclaim to avoid.

Evidence note

Record the source, date, issuing health organization or qualified professional body, and whether the claim is general education, public-health guidance or individualized medical advice.

Reflection

What can a learner safely do for themselves here, and what requires professional help or formal certification?
