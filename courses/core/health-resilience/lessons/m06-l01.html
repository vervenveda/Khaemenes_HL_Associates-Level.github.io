Mood, Anxiety and Functioning

Describe common experiences without labeling oneself or others with diagnoses.

Safety boundary

This lesson is educational only. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe. Urgent symptoms, emergencies, self-harm risk, severe distress, poisoning, overdose, serious injury or other immediate danger require qualified emergency or medical support.

Concept frame

How to discuss mental health without stigma or amateur diagnosis.

Practice
