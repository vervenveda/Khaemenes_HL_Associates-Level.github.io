Associate-Level Shared Collegiate Core · 3 Learning Units

Personal Health, Wellbeing & Resilience

A 45-hour health-literacy course covering everyday wellbeing, prevention, safety, emergency readiness, help-seeking and evidence-based evaluation of health claims.

Professional boundary

Educational only. This course does not qualify a learner to diagnose, treat, prescribe, provide clinical counseling, perform professional first aid, or practice a regulated health profession.
