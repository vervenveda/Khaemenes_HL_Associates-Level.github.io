CORE-106 Readiness Diagnostic

Readiness evidence. This assessment is educational and does not create professional health authority.

1. Explain Health Information Versus Medical Advice: Distinguish general education from diagnosis, treatment and individualized clinical advice. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

2. Explain Trusted Sources and Red Flags: Evaluate public-health, clinical and commercial health information. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

3. Explain When to Seek Professional Help: Recognize situations that require qualified medical, mental-health or emergency support. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

4. Explain Sleep Need and Consistency: Understand regular sleep timing and adequate rest. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

5. Explain Sleep Environment and Habits: Identify behaviors and environments that support better sleep. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

6. Explain Fatigue, Safety and Help-Seeking: Recognize when persistent fatigue or sleep problems warrant professional evaluation. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

7. Explain Balanced Eating Patterns: Use broad food-group and nutrient principles rather than restrictive fad rules. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

8. Explain Hydration and Context: Adjust hydration awareness for activity, environment and individual needs. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

9. Explain Reading Food Claims and Labels: Interpret labels and marketing without treating a single nutrient as the whole diet. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

10. Explain Activity, Mobility and Daily Movement: Build regular movement appropriate to ability and context. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

11. Explain Strength, Endurance and Recovery: Understand broad training categories and recovery needs. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

12. Explain Injury Prevention and Warning Signs: Recognize when pain, injury or symptoms require rest or professional evaluation. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

13. Explain Stress Response and Load: Distinguish short-term stress from sustained overload. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

14. Explain Grounding, Breathing and Recovery Practices: Use low-risk self-regulation strategies for everyday stress. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

15. Explain When Coping Is Not Enough: Recognize signs that professional mental-health support may be needed. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

16. Explain Mood, Anxiety and Functioning: Describe common experiences without labeling oneself or others with diagnoses. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

17. Explain Support Networks and Trusted Help: Identify appropriate personal, school, community and clinical support. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

18. Explain Crisis Recognition and Emergency Help: Know that immediate danger, self-harm risk or severe crisis requires urgent qualified help. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

19. Explain Emergency Action Priorities: Recognize scene safety, emergency contact and basic response priorities. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.

20. Explain First Aid Scope and Certification Boundaries: Distinguish awareness education from certified first-aid or CPR training. Include one reliable source, one safety boundary and one situation that requires professional help.
