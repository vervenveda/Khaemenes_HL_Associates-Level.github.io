Health Literacy & Safety Rubric

  1. Health claims are supported by appropriate sources.
  2. Education is not presented as diagnosis or treatment.
  3. Emergency/help-seeking thresholds are recognized.
  4. Professional/certification boundaries are accurate.
  5. Risk and uncertainty are communicated clearly.
  6. Traditional/wellness practices are evaluated respectfully and critically.
  7. No supplement, medication or intervention is recommended as individualized treatment.
  8. Personal plan is realistic and safety-conscious.
  9. Privacy and sensitive information are protected.
  10. Reflection identifies where qualified support is needed.
