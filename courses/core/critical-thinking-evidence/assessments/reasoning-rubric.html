Critical Reasoning Rubric

  1. Claim is clearly stated.
  2. Definitions and scope are explicit.
  3. Evidence is relevant and accurately represented.
  4. Inference from evidence to conclusion is explained.
  5. Alternative explanations or counterarguments are addressed.
  6. Causal claims distinguish correlation from mechanism.
  7. Probability/risk claims use appropriate denominators and uncertainty.
  8. Source expertise and incentives are evaluated without ad hominem reasoning.
  9. Uncertainty and confidence are calibrated.
  10. Revision conditions are stated: what evidence would change the conclusion?
