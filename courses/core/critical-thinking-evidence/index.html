Associate-Level Shared Collegiate Core · 3 Learning Units

Critical Thinking, Logic & Evidence

A 45-hour course in argument structure, evidence quality, causation, fallacies, probability, expertise, decision-making, bias and self-correction.

Course outcomes

  • Map arguments into claims, reasons, evidence and assumptions.
  • Distinguish validity, soundness, inductive strength and uncertainty.
  • Evaluate evidence types and source expertise.
  • Recognize common fallacies without using fallacy labels as substitutes for analysis.
  • Reason carefully about causation, risk and probability.
  • Represent opposing views fairly and revise beliefs when evidence changes.
