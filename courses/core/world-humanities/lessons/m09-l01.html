KHL-CORE-108 · Module 09 · Lesson 01

Travel Writing and the Gaze

Examine how travelers describe unfamiliar peoples and places.

Humanities target

Interpret works and practices through evidence, comparison and context rather than treating one culture, translation or canon as the default measure of all others.

Concept frame

How travel, migration, geography and exchange shape cultural interpretation.

Practice

Choose one text, artwork, artifact, practice or historical source that illustrates examine how travelers describe unfamiliar peoples and places.
Describe the source closely before interpreting it.
Identify the historical, cultural, religious, linguistic or institutional context that materially affects interpretation.
Compare a second perspective, translation, tradition or source without treating difference as deficiency.
State one uncertainty, missing voice or limitation in the surviving evidence.

Source dossier

Record creator/author if known, title or description, date/period, place/culture, medium/language, edition/translation if relevant, source location, and one contextual question.

Reflection

How did context or comparison change your first interpretation?
