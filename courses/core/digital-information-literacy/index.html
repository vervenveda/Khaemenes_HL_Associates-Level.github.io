Associate-Level Shared Collegiate Core · 3 Learning Units

Digital Literacy, Information Verification & Research Tools

A 45-hour course in digital organization, search, verification, privacy, security, accessibility, productivity tools, reproducibility and responsible AI use.

12 Modules · 36 Lessons

  1. Digital Systems & File Literacy
    • Files, Folders and Naming
    • Formats, Compatibility and Preservation
    • Local, Cloud and Backup Models
  2. Search & Information Retrieval
    • Search Intent and Query Design
    • Operators, Filters and Domain Search
    • Search Logs and Retrieval Trails
  3. Source Verification
    • Authorship, Publication and Provenance
    • Cross-Checking Claims
    • Images, Screenshots and Context Loss
  4. Misinformation, Manipulation & Scams
    • Common Scam Patterns
    • Manipulative Interface Patterns
    • Rumors, Virality and Emotional Amplification
  5. Privacy, Security & Identity
    • Passwords, Passphrases and Authentication
    • Permissions, Tracking and Data Sharing
    • Phishing, Links and Attachment Safety
  6. Digital Citizenship & Communication
    • Tone, Context and Audience Online
    • Boundaries, Harassment and Reporting
    • Permanent, Searchable and Shareable Records
  7. Accessible Digital Work
    • Readable Structure and Headings
    • Alt Text, Captions and Transcripts
    • Contrast, Keyboard Access and Simple Layout
  8. Documents, Spreadsheets & Data Basics
    • Document Structure and Revision
    • Spreadsheet Literacy
    • Data Export and Interoperability
  9. Research Tools & Reproducibility
    • Reference and Note Systems
    • Version Control Concepts
    • Reproducible Research Packages
  10. AI & Automated Tools
    • AI as Assistant, Not Authority
    • Verification, Hallucination and Source Checking
    • Disclosure, Attribution and Academic Integrity
  11. Digital Workflows & Collaboration
    • Roles, Permissions and Shared Folders
    • Comments, Tasks and Decision Records
    • Conflict, Merge and Handoff Practices
  12. Integrated Digital Research Practice
    • Research Workspace Design
    • Verification & Accessibility Audit
    • Capstone Digital Research Package

Assessment

Diagnostic · module checks · midterm · final · digital research package · verification/accessibility audit. Required knowledge gates use the 80% mastery floor.
