CORE-104 Midterm

Cumulative Modules 1–6. Demonstrations and project evidence require evaluator review.

1. Demonstrate or explain Files, Folders and Naming: Organize digital work with stable names, folders and versions. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

2. Demonstrate or explain Formats, Compatibility and Preservation: Choose formats that support access, editing, printing and long-term readability. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

3. Demonstrate or explain Local, Cloud and Backup Models: Distinguish working copies, backups, sync and archival storage. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

4. Demonstrate or explain Search Intent and Query Design: Translate a research need into precise search terms and phrases. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

5. Demonstrate or explain Operators, Filters and Domain Search: Use search operators and filters without assuming ranking equals quality. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

6. Demonstrate or explain Search Logs and Retrieval Trails: Record what was searched, found, rejected and revisited. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

7. Demonstrate or explain Authorship, Publication and Provenance: Identify who created a source, where it originated and whether it was altered. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

8. Demonstrate or explain Cross-Checking Claims: Compare independent sources rather than relying on repetition. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

9. Demonstrate or explain Images, Screenshots and Context Loss: Recognize cropped, recycled or decontextualized media. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

10. Demonstrate or explain Common Scam Patterns: Recognize urgency, impersonation, payment pressure and credential theft. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

11. Demonstrate or explain Manipulative Interface Patterns: Identify dark patterns, misleading buttons and consent pressure. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

12. Demonstrate or explain Rumors, Virality and Emotional Amplification: Pause before sharing highly arousing or weakly sourced claims. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

13. Demonstrate or explain Passwords, Passphrases and Authentication: Use unique credentials and multi-factor authentication where available. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

14. Demonstrate or explain Permissions, Tracking and Data Sharing: Evaluate what apps and sites request access to. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

15. Demonstrate or explain Phishing, Links and Attachment Safety: Inspect senders, destinations and unexpected files before acting. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

16. Demonstrate or explain Tone, Context and Audience Online: Adapt communication to public, academic and private settings. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

17. Demonstrate or explain Boundaries, Harassment and Reporting: Use blocking, reporting and documentation tools appropriately. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

18. Demonstrate or explain Permanent, Searchable and Shareable Records: Understand that digital posts can persist beyond intended audiences. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

19. Demonstrate or explain Readable Structure and Headings: Use semantic headings, lists and descriptive labels. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.

20. Demonstrate or explain Alt Text, Captions and Transcripts: Provide text equivalents for visual and audio information. Include one verification, security, privacy, accessibility or reproducibility check.
