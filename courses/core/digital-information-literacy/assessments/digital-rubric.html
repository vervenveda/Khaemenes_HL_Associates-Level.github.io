Digital Practice Rubric

  1. Files and versions are organized and recoverable.
  2. Sources are traceable to original context.
  3. Search/research trail is documented.
  4. Privacy and permissions are appropriate.
  5. Security risks are identified and mitigated.
  6. Documents/media are accessible.
  7. Data transformations are transparent.
  8. Automated/AI output is independently verified.
  9. Attribution and disclosure rules are followed.
  10. Another reviewer can navigate and reproduce the work.
