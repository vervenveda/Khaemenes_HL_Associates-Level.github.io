Associate-Level Shared Collegiate Core · 3 Learning Units

Academic Language, Research & College Communication

A 45-hour foundation in precise academic language, source literacy, research strategy, argument, synthesis, citation, revision and scholarly communication.

Credential boundary

This course contributes 3 internal Khaemenes learning units toward the free, non-accredited Associate-Level Certificate. These units are not institutional college credits.

Course outcomes

  • Read academic sources for claims, evidence, structure and context.
  • Evaluate source authority, perspective, reliability and limitations.
  • Develop focused research questions and documented search strategies.
  • Quote, paraphrase, summarize and cite without obscuring source ownership.
  • Write evidence-based arguments that address counterarguments and uncertainty.
  • Synthesize multiple sources into coherent scholarly communication.
  • Revise for reasoning, clarity, style and integrity.
  • Present and defend a research brief and source portfolio.
