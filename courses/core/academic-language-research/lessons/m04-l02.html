KHL-CORE-101 · Module 04 · Lesson 02

Paragraph Logic & Signposting

Identify how paragraphs develop and connect ideas.

Learning target

Demonstrate the skill accurately in reading, research and writing, and preserve evidence that another reviewer can inspect.

Concept frame

How skilled readers identify argument, organization, key terms and implied reasoning.

Core principle: Academic communication is not difficult language for its own sake. It is language chosen so evidence, reasoning, uncertainty and attribution are visible to the reader.

Guided practice

Define the central skill in Paragraph Logic & Signposting and explain why it matters in college-level research or communication.
Apply the skill to a short example from science, civics, health, business or the arts.
Identify a weak or misleading use of this skill and revise it.

Independent application

Create one piece of portfolio evidence that demonstrates identify how paragraphs develop and connect ideas.
Explain how this lesson connects to evidence, source integrity or scholarly communication.

Source practice

Select a credible source connected to one of the five Associate-Level tracks. Record author/creator, title, date if available, publisher/site, URL or physical location, the specific claim you are using, and one limitation or question about the source.

Writing evidence

Write 150–300 words applying today’s skill. Separate source language from your own interpretation and label uncertainty when the evidence does not justify certainty.

Reflection

What changed in your interpretation or writing after applying this skill? What would a skeptical reader still need to know?
