Writing & Research Evidence Rubric

Required dimensions

  1. Purpose & claim — focused, appropriately qualified, responsive to the task.
  2. Evidence — relevant, sufficient, accurately represented.
  3. Source literacy — authority, context, limitations and attribution addressed.
  4. Reasoning — connections between evidence and conclusions are explicit.
  5. Synthesis — sources are integrated around ideas rather than listed sequentially.
  6. Counterargument & uncertainty — competing reasoning and limits are represented fairly.
  7. Organization — structure and paragraph logic guide the reader.
  8. Language & style — precise, readable, discipline-appropriate.
  9. Citation integrity — quotation, paraphrase and citation preserve source ownership.
  10. Revision evidence — substantive changes respond to feedback or identified weaknesses.

Course completion requires satisfactory evidence in every dimension; a strong average does not compensate for plagiarism, fabricated sources, or missing attribution.
