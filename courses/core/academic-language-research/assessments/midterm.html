CORE-101 Midterm

Cumulative Modules 1–6 assessment.

Knowledge gates require 80% where objectively scored. Written responses require evaluator review. Browser-local completion alone is not formal evidence.

1. Apply and explain observation, inference & claim: Distinguish what is directly observed from what is inferred and claimed. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

2. Apply and explain corroboration & context: Use independent evidence and context to strengthen or limit a claim. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

3. Apply and explain qualification, nuance & scope: Write claims that accurately match the strength and limits of available evidence. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

4. Apply and explain roots, prefixes & suffixes: Analyze meaningful word parts and use morphology to infer unfamiliar academic terms. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

5. Apply and explain derivation & word families: Track how grammatical form and meaning change across related words. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

6. Apply and explain polysemy across disciplines: Compare how one word can carry related but distinct meanings in different fields. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

7. Apply and explain formal and informal register: Recognize register and revise language for scholarly contexts. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

8. Apply and explain audience, purpose & tone: Align vocabulary, explanation and tone with a defined audience and purpose. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

9. Apply and explain precision without pretension: Prefer clear, specific language over unnecessary complexity or vague abstraction. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

10. Apply and explain close reading & annotation: Annotate key claims, terms, evidence, definitions and questions in a source. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

11. Apply and explain paragraph logic & signposting: Identify how paragraphs develop and connect ideas. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

12. Apply and explain summary versus analysis: Separate faithful summary from interpretation, evaluation and response. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

13. Apply and explain primary, secondary & tertiary sources: Classify source types and choose them for appropriate research purposes. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

14. Apply and explain authority, expertise & context: Evaluate source authority without treating credentials as automatic proof. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

15. Apply and explain bias, perspective & reliability: Analyze perspective, incentives, omissions and evidentiary reliability. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

16. Apply and explain from topic to research question: Narrow a subject into a question with manageable scope and clear concepts. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

17. Apply and explain keywords, synonyms & search logic: Build search vocabulary using related terms, controlled phrases and Boolean logic. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

18. Apply and explain research trail & search revision: Document searches, revise terms and explain why sources were kept or rejected. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

19. Apply and explain quotation, paraphrase & summary: Choose among quotation, paraphrase and summary based on purpose. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence

20. Apply and explain avoiding patchwriting & misrepresentation: Paraphrase conceptually rather than replacing isolated words. Include evidence or a concrete example where appropriate.

Response / evaluator evidence
