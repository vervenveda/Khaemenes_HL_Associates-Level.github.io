Financial Literacy Rubric

  1. Income and expenses are realistic and documented.
  2. Interest, fees and total cost are calculated correctly.
  3. Credit/debt tradeoffs are identified.
  4. Tax/insurance statements stay within educational scope.
  5. Emergency and liquidity risks are considered.
  6. Investment claims acknowledge risk and uncertainty.
  7. Consumer/fraud protections are included.
  8. Major purchases use total-cost comparisons.
  9. Scenarios test changing assumptions.
  10. Plan identifies when professional advice may be appropriate.
