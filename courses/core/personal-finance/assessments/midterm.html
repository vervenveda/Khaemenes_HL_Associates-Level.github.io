CORE-107 Midterm

Cumulative Modules 1–6. Show assumptions, calculations, sources and relevant consumer/advice boundaries.

2. Explain or calculate Income, Net Pay and Cash Flow: Distinguish gross income, net income and recurring cash flow. Include one risk, fee, limitation or source-verification step.

13. Explain or calculate Debt Inventory and Prioritization: List balances, rates, payments, consequences and options. Include one risk, fee, limitation or source-verification step.
