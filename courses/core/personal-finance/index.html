Associate-Level Shared Collegiate Core · 3 Learning Units

Personal Finance, Economic Life & Consumer Decisions

A 45-hour practical financial-literacy course covering cash flow, banking, credit, debt, taxes, insurance, saving, investing basics, major purchases, fraud and long-term planning.

Advice boundary

Educational only. The course does not provide individualized financial, investment, tax, insurance or legal advice.

12 Modules · 36 Lessons

  1. Financial Foundations & Goals
    • Needs, Wants and Financial Priorities
    • Income, Net Pay and Cash Flow
    • Short-, Medium- and Long-Term Goals
  2. Budgeting & Spending Systems
    • Building a Working Budget
    • Tracking, Variance and Adjustment
    • Sinking Funds and Irregular Costs
  3. Banking & Payment Systems
    • Checking, Savings and Account Features
    • Cards, Transfers and Digital Payments
    • Statements, Reconciliation and Errors
  4. Credit & Borrowing
    • Credit Reports and Credit Scores
    • Interest, APR and Borrowing Cost
    • Responsible Credit Use and Warning Signs
  5. Debt Strategy & Consumer Protection
    • Debt Inventory and Prioritization
    • Collections, Rights and Documentation
    • Debt Relief Claims and Scams
  6. Taxes & Public Finance Basics
    • Income Taxes and Withholding
    • Payroll, Sales and Property Taxes
    • Records, Deadlines and Professional Help
  7. Insurance & Risk Management
    • Risk, Premiums and Deductibles
    • Health, Auto, Renters/Home and Life Coverage
    • Exclusions, Limits and Claims
  8. Saving & Emergency Reserves
    • Emergency Funds and Liquidity
    • Interest and Compound Growth
    • Automation and Behavioral Design
  9. Investing Foundations
    • Risk, Return and Time Horizon
    • Diversification and Concentration Risk
    • Fees, Fraud and Investment Claims
  10. Housing, Transportation & Major Purchases
    • Renting, Owning and Total Cost
    • Transportation Cost of Ownership
    • Financing Major Purchases
  11. Consumer Decisions, Fraud & Identity
    • Comparison Shopping and Total Cost
    • Fraud, Identity Theft and Account Security
    • Contracts, Subscriptions and Cancellation Terms
  12. Integrated Personal Finance Plan
    • Personal Financial Snapshot
    • Scenario and Stress Testing
    • Capstone Financial Plan & Reflection

Assessment

Diagnostic · module checks · midterm · final · personal financial plan · scenario stress test · consumer-protection checklist.
