KHL-CORE-107 · Module 11 · Lesson 03

Contracts, Subscriptions and Cancellation Terms

Read key obligations before agreeing to recurring services or financing.

Financial literacy target

Make decisions by comparing total cost, risk, time horizon and contractual obligations rather than relying on sales language or single-number shortcuts.

Boundary

This course is educational only and does not provide individualized financial, investment, tax, insurance or legal advice.

Concept frame

How to protect money and personal information in marketplaces.

Practice

Explain read key obligations before agreeing to recurring services or financing. using a realistic example.
Identify one fee, risk, assumption or contractual term that could materially change the decision.
Compare at least two options using total cost or long-term effect rather than headline price alone.
Name one official or qualified source a learner should consult for current rules or individualized guidance.
State one scam, conflict of interest or misleading claim associated with this topic.

Evidence artifact

Preserve a calculation, comparison table, statement review, budget scenario, contract checklist or source note that another reviewer can inspect.

Reflection

Which assumption would most change this decision if it turned out to be wrong?
