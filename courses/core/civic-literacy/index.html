Associate-Level Shared Collegiate Core · 3 Learning Units

Constitutional, Civic & Community Literacy

A 45-hour nonpartisan civic foundation in constitutional structure, rights, institutions, public evidence, legal reasoning, participation, policy and community inquiry.

Boundary

This is civic education, not legal advice or professional legal training. Learners examine institutions and competing interpretations without being assigned a partisan position.

12 Modules · 36 Lessons

  1. Constitutional Foundations
    • Constitution, Charter and Foundational Law
    • Popular Sovereignty and Legitimate Authority
    • Federalism, Separation and Shared Power
  2. Institutions & Checks
    • Legislative Power and Representation
    • Executive Power and Administration
    • Judicial Review and Constitutional Interpretation
  3. Rights, Liberties & the Bill of Rights
    • Speech, Religion, Assembly and Petition
    • Privacy, Search, Due Process and Criminal Procedure
    • Unenumerated Rights, Reserved Powers and Constitutional Structure
  4. Equal Protection & Civil Rights
    • Equal Protection and Legal Classification
    • Civil Rights Movements and Constitutional Change
    • Rights in Practice: Access, Enforcement and Remedy
  5. Law, Regulation & Public Institutions
    • Statutes, Regulations and Administrative Rules
    • Local Government and Public Services
    • Public Records, Meetings and Transparency
  6. Elections, Representation & Participation
    • Voting, Districts and Representation
    • Political Parties, Interest Groups and Civil Society
    • Petition, Comment, Testimony and Public Participation
  7. Civil Discourse & Pluralism
    • Rights, Responsibilities and Civic Norms
    • Disagreement, Protest and Public Order
    • Dialogue Across Difference
  8. Law, Evidence & Legal Reasoning
    • Facts, Rules, Issues and Holdings
    • Precedent, Analogy and Distinguishing Cases
    • Text, History, Purpose and Competing Interpretations
  9. Civic Information & Media
    • Primary Public Sources
    • News, Commentary and Institutional Claims
    • Misinformation and Civic Verification
  10. Public Policy & Tradeoffs
    • Problem Definition and Stakeholders
    • Policy Options, Costs and Distribution
    • Implementation, Evaluation and Unintended Effects
  11. Community, Service & Local Inquiry
    • Community Asset and Institution Mapping
    • Public-Service Research
    • Volunteerism, Mutual Aid and Ethical Service
  12. Integrated Civic Inquiry
    • Constitutional Issue Brief
    • Public Communication & Civic Proposal
    • Capstone Civic Defense

Assessment

Diagnostic · module checks · midterm · final · constitutional/public-policy brief · community evidence dossier · civic presentation/defense.
