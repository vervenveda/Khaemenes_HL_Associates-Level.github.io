Civic Inquiry Rubric

  1. Constitutional/institutional question is clearly framed.
  2. Primary sources are accurately cited.
  3. Legal claims are separated from policy preferences.
  4. Competing interpretations are represented fairly.
  5. Historical and institutional context is included.
  6. Evidence quality and limitations are discussed.
  7. Stakeholders and tradeoffs are identified.
  8. Public communication is clear and civil.
  9. Uncertainty is acknowledged where appropriate.
  10. No claim of legal advice or professional authority is made.
