CORE-105 Readiness Diagnostic

Readiness evidence. Use primary/official sources where appropriate and separate legal, factual and value claims.

1. Explain or analyze Constitution, Charter and Foundational Law: Distinguish a constitution from ordinary statutes, policies and customs. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

2. Explain or analyze Popular Sovereignty and Legitimate Authority: Explain how political authority is justified and constrained. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

3. Explain or analyze Federalism, Separation and Shared Power: Map divided authority across branches and levels of government. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

4. Explain or analyze Legislative Power and Representation: Understand lawmaking, representation and institutional design. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

5. Explain or analyze Executive Power and Administration: Distinguish executive authority, implementation and administrative power. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

6. Explain or analyze Judicial Review and Constitutional Interpretation: Explain courts, review, precedent and interpretive disagreement. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

7. Explain or analyze Speech, Religion, Assembly and Petition: Analyze First Amendment freedoms and their limits. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

8. Explain or analyze Privacy, Search, Due Process and Criminal Procedure: Connect Fourth–Eighth Amendment protections to procedural fairness. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

9. Explain or analyze Unenumerated Rights, Reserved Powers and Constitutional Structure: Interpret the Ninth and Tenth Amendments within federalism and rights debates. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

10. Explain or analyze Equal Protection and Legal Classification: Distinguish equality principles from identical treatment. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

11. Explain or analyze Civil Rights Movements and Constitutional Change: Connect legal doctrine with civic action and institutional response. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

12. Explain or analyze Rights in Practice: Access, Enforcement and Remedy: Separate formal rights from practical access and enforcement. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

13. Explain or analyze Statutes, Regulations and Administrative Rules: Distinguish major forms of public law and rulemaking. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

14. Explain or analyze Local Government and Public Services: Map municipal, county and local institutional responsibilities. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

15. Explain or analyze Public Records, Meetings and Transparency: Understand basic transparency, accountability and access principles. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

16. Explain or analyze Voting, Districts and Representation: Examine electoral structure, representation and apportionment. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

17. Explain or analyze Political Parties, Interest Groups and Civil Society: Understand organized participation beyond formal government. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

18. Explain or analyze Petition, Comment, Testimony and Public Participation: Practice lawful civic participation in public decision-making. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

19. Explain or analyze Rights, Responsibilities and Civic Norms: Distinguish legal rights from civic expectations and responsibilities. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.

20. Explain or analyze Disagreement, Protest and Public Order: Analyze tensions among expression, assembly, safety and institutional response. Identify the relevant institution/source and one limitation or competing interpretation.
