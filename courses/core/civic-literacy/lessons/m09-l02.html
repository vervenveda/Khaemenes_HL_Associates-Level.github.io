KHL-CORE-105 · Module 09 · Lesson 02

News, Commentary and Institutional Claims

Separate reporting, opinion, advocacy and official statements.

Civic target

Use constitutional structure, primary sources and evidence to understand public institutions without confusing civic education with legal advice.

Concept frame

How citizens verify public claims and institutional information.

Practice

Explain separate reporting, opinion, advocacy and official statements. in plain language.
Locate one primary or official source relevant to this topic and record its origin.
Identify one common oversimplification or misconception about this topic.
Apply the topic to a current or historical civic example without assuming one partisan conclusion.
State one factual, legal or value question that would need separate evidence.

Primary-source evidence

Record the title, issuing institution, date, relevant passage or section, and why the source is authoritative for the specific claim you are making.

Reflection

Which part of this issue is constitutional/legal, which part is factual, and which part is a value or policy judgment?
