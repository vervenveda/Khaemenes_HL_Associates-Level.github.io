Bias, Nonresponse and Convenience Samples

Identify common pathways from sampling design to misleading conclusions.

Quantitative target

Use numbers to clarify rather than decorate an argument. Every result should preserve units, denominator, population, assumptions and uncertainty where relevant.

Concept frame

How data quality depends on who or what is measured and how.

Practice

Data note
