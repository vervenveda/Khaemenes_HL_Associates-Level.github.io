Quantitative Evidence Rubric

  1. Question and population are clearly defined.
  2. Data source and provenance are documented.
  3. Variables, units and denominators are explicit.
  4. Calculations are correct and reproducible.
  5. Tables/graphs match the data type and avoid distortion.
  6. Center/spread/association are interpreted in context.
  7. Causal claims do not exceed the design.
  8. Uncertainty and limitations are stated.
  9. Risk/statistical significance are not exaggerated.
  10. Conclusion answers the question without false precision.
