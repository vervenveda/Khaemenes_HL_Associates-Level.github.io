Associate-Level Shared Collegiate Core · 3 Learning Units

Quantitative Reasoning & Statistics

A 45-hour general-education course in numeracy, rates, graphs, distributions, probability, sampling, risk, inference, uncertainty and data-informed decisions.

Scope

This is the shared quantitative-literacy course for every concentration. It does not replace the full Higher Learning Statistics course; learners who need deeper statistical methods may continue into that course.

12 Modules · 36 Lessons

  1. Numbers, Ratios & Rates
    • Units, Magnitude and Estimation
    • Ratios, Rates and Proportions
    • Percent Change and Percentage Points
  2. Tables, Graphs & Visual Evidence
    • Reading Tables and Structured Data
    • Axes, Scales and Graph Integrity
    • Choosing Effective Displays
  3. Describing Distributions
    • Mean, Median and Mode
    • Range, IQR and Standard Deviation
    • Shape, Outliers and Distribution Stories
  4. Sampling & Measurement
    • Population, Sample and Sampling Frame
    • Bias, Nonresponse and Convenience Samples
    • Measurement, Variables and Operational Definitions
  5. Probability & Uncertainty
    • Events, Complements and Simple Probability
    • Conditional Probability and Independence
    • Expected Value and Long-Run Reasoning
  6. Relationships Between Variables
    • Scatterplots and Association
    • Correlation and Its Limits
    • Confounding and Alternative Explanations
  7. Risk, Rates & Public Claims
    • Absolute Risk and Relative Risk
    • Base Rates and Denominators
    • Per-Capita and Standardized Comparisons
  8. Inference & Margin of Error
    • Sampling Variability
    • Confidence Intervals in Plain Language
    • Margin of Error and Sample Size
  9. Hypothesis Tests & Practical Significance
    • Null Models and P-Values
    • Statistical Versus Practical Significance
    • Multiple Testing and Selective Reporting
  10. Data Ethics & Reproducibility
    • Data Provenance and Documentation
    • Privacy, Consent and Harm
    • Reproducible Analysis and Transparent Methods
  11. Quantitative Decision-Making
    • Cost, Benefit and Tradeoff Tables
    • Forecasts, Scenarios and Sensitivity
    • Communicating Uncertainty to Non-Specialists
  12. Integrated Data Inquiry
    • Question, Data and Analysis Plan
    • Analysis Brief and Visual Evidence
    • Capstone Quantitative Defense

Assessment

Diagnostic · module checks · midterm · final · quantitative investigation · visual evidence · analysis brief · defense. 80% mastery floor for required knowledge gates.
