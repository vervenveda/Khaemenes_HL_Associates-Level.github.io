About ARTS-141

Teach the research, critique, documentation and writing skills that make studio work understandable and professionally presentable.

Course Features

Artist Research

Use museums, galleries, archives, exhibitions and reliable sources to inform studio thinking.

Critique & Revision

Practice structured feedback, diagnosis and revision without making the Academy the final taste authority.

Portfolio Documentation

Build professional artwork records, captions, metadata, provenance and curated evidence.

Professional Writing

Develop artist statements, bios, proposals, applications and correspondence.
