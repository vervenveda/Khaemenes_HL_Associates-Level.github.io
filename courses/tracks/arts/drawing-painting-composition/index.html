About ARTS-211

Year 2 deepens drawing, painting, composition, medium control, sustained original series development, professional studio practice, and artist-facing business work.

Course Features

Sustained Studio Practice

Projects develop across a full 36-week second-year sequence.

Medium-Specific Technique

Drawing and painting skills are strengthened through deliberate practice.

Professional Artist Work

CEO, artrepreneur, marketing, and promotion are integrated weekly.

Portfolio & Mastery

Evidence, vocabulary, quizzes, and major assessments support the 80% gate.
