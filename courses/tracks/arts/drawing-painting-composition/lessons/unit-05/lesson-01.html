Structure of the Head

ARTS-211 · Unit 05 · Week 12 · Creative Day

Learner ProfileParent ProfileEnrollment

About This Creative Day

Construct the head from large masses and axes, compare feature placement and proportion, then revise one structural relationship from observed or referenced evidence.

Observe → research → experiment → create/revise → reflect/archive.

Creative Day Goals

Structure

Establish large relationships before detail.

Observe

Use evidence rather than generic symbols.

Develop

Create a sustained portrait/figure study.

Revise

Make one evidence-based correction.

Materials & Accessible Alternative

Primary Tools

Use drawing, painting or digital media already available.

Reference / Subject

Use direct observation, consent-safe self/peer reference, mannequin, mirror, public-domain or learner-created reference as appropriate.

Evidence Method

Camera/scanner optional; written/drawn documentation counts.

No-Purchase Path

Ordinary paper and pencil fully satisfy the structural work.

Creative Action Steps
01 · Mass Block-In
Establish cranial and jaw masses.
02 · Axis
Add centerline and brow line to show head direction.
03 · Landmarks
Place major structural reference points before features.
04 · Feature Placement
Locate eyes, nose, mouth and ears relative to the construction.
05 · Develop
Create one observed/reference head study.
06 · Revise
Correct one mass, axis or placement issue.
Required Lesson Evidence
Vocabulary
cranial mass · jaw mass · centerline · brow line · proportion · landmark · feature placement · construction
Reflection & Archive

Archive studies, developed work and revision together as Week 12 evidence.
