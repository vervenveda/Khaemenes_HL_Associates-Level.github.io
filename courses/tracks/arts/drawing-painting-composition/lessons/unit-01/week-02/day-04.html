About This Artpreneur Day

Turn the Week 2 process into a professional before/after portfolio record. The value of the asset is not a perfect drawing; it is the visible proof that observation, comparison and revision changed the work.

Year 2 follows the Bazaar Art studio sequence: observe → research → experiment → create/revise → reflect/archive. Artistic taste is never scored.
