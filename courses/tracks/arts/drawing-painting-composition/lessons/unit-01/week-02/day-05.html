About This Marketing & Promotion Day

Present the Week 2 observation-correction record to one appropriate viewer and test whether the before/after change is understandable. The viewer is evaluating clarity of evidence—not artistic taste, attractiveness or popularity.

Year 2 follows the Bazaar Art studio sequence: observe → research → experiment → create/revise → reflect/archive. Artistic taste is never scored.

Marketing & Promotion Day Goals

Present the Process

Show memory sketch, observed drawing and revision together.

Test Clarity

Ask what changed and what evidence seems to justify it.

Separate Taste from Signal

Record useful structural feedback without scoring preference.

Carry Learning Forward

Choose one seeing habit to continue into Week 3.

Required Marketing Evidence
Signal vs Vanity Metrics

Useful Signal

Specific comment about angle, edge, spacing, proportion or negative space.

Weak Signal

General praise/dislike without observable detail.

Vanity Metric

Likes, followers, impressions and virality.
