About This Skills & Technique Day

Wednesday isolates three seeing skills that reduce symbol drawing: edge tracking, angle comparison and negative-space measurement. You will practice them in small controlled studies, retry one weak relationship and transfer the result into the active Week 2 drawing.

Year 2 follows the Bazaar Art studio sequence: observe → research → experiment → create/revise → reflect/archive. Artistic taste is never scored.

Skills & Technique Day Goals

Track Edges

Follow actual boundary changes rather than naming the object.

Compare Angles

Test slants against vertical, horizontal and nearby edges.

Measure Empty Shapes

Use negative space to check placement and proportion.

Transfer

Apply one corrected observation to Monday’s work.

Skill of the Day

Edge Tracking

Follow the subject boundary without translating it into a named symbol.

Angle Comparison

Compare slants against vertical/horizontal and nearby edges.

Negative Space

Measure empty shapes as carefully as the object itself.

Transfer

Apply one correction to the Week 2 observed drawing.
