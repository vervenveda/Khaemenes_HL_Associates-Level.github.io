Revision Through Composition

ARTS-211 · Unit 04 · Week 11 · Creative Day

Learner ProfileParent ProfileEnrollment

About This Creative Day

Take an earlier composition, diagnose one structural weakness, create at least three recomposition options, and produce a revised version that changes hierarchy, balance or spatial organization deliberately.

Observe → research → experiment → create/revise → reflect/archive.

Creative Day Goals

Generate

Create real structural alternatives.

Compare

Judge hierarchy and spatial relationships.

Develop

Choose one option intentionally.

Revise

Make one evidence-based structural correction.

Materials & Accessible Alternative

Primary Tools

Use drawing, painting, collage or digital media already available.

Subject / Problem

Choose one simple subject or visual problem suitable for composition comparison.

Comparison Aid

Use a viewfinder, thumbnail boxes or digital crop frame if helpful.

No-Purchase Path

Paper and pencil fully satisfy the structural work.

Creative Action Steps
01 · Select Baseline
Choose an earlier composition with a specific structural weakness.
02 · Diagnose
Name the hierarchy/balance/spatial problem.
03 · Recompose
Create at least three revised arrangements.
04 · Compare
Choose the option that best addresses the diagnosis.
05 · Develop
Produce the revised composition.
06 · Archive
Keep original, options and final revision together.
