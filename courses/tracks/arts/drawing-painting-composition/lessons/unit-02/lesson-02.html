Charcoal: Mass, Atmosphere & Revision

ARTS-211 · Drawing, Painting & Composition · Unit 02 · Week 04 · Creative Day

Learner ProfileParent ProfileEnrollment

About This Creative Day

Build a charcoal drawing from large dark/light masses before detail, use additive and subtractive methods, control atmospheric edges, and document one lift-out or re-block revision.

Year 2 follows observe → research → experiment → create/revise → reflect/archive. Artistic taste is never scored.

Creative Day Goals

Control the Medium

Use the week’s medium deliberately rather than automatically.

Observe Relationships

Base decisions on edges, values, spacing and form evidence.

Experiment

Create controlled tests before committing to the developed study.

Revise

Make one visible evidence-based change.

Materials & Accessible Alternative

Primary Tools

vine/compressed charcoal or soft graphite substitute; paper; kneaded/ordinary eraser

Observed Subject

Choose a simple form or still life with clear light, overlap and edge changes.

Evidence Method

Camera/scanner optional; written/drawn documentation counts.

No-Purchase Path

Use equivalent materials already available; specialist supplies are never required.

Creative Action Steps
01 · Mass Block-InCover large dark/light shapes before contour detail.
02 · Additive PassBuild darks with side-of-stick, broad strokes or soft-value equivalent.
03 · Subtractive PassLift light with eraser or digital subtractive tool.
04 · AtmosphereVary edge clarity so not every boundary is equally sharp.
05 · RevisionRe-block or lift one area where detail disrupted the major mass.
