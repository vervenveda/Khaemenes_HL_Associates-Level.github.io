About This Artpreneur Day

Today you turn the Week 5 hue, value, and saturation studies plus the final artwork into a professional process record. The asset should make the controlled comparisons visible and show how one study informed the final color decision.

Artpreneur Day supports Week 05 · Hue, Value and Saturation.

Artpreneur Day Goals

Show the Three Studies

Present hue, value, and saturation comparisons clearly.

Document Constants

Explain what stayed the same across all studies.

Explain the Choice

Show why one study became the basis for the larger final artwork.

Archive the Color Record

Store the master page and source images for later portfolio use.

Business Asset of the Day

Asset

One Color Study Process Portfolio Record.

Purpose

Show controlled color experimentation and the reasoning behind the final palette.

Format

Printable page, PDF, image export, or offline digital portfolio page.

Integrity Rule

Preserve all three studies; do not hide unsuccessful comparisons that explain the final choice.

Requirements & Finished Example
Required · Hue StudyInclude Study A and identify the hue changes.
Required · Value StudyInclude Study B and identify the light-dark structure.
Required · Saturation StudyInclude Study C and identify vivid versus muted choices.
Required · Final ArtworkInclude the larger work based on the chosen study.
Required · Rationale & MetadataRecord subject, date, medium/tool, palette, lesson linkage, and why the chosen study was developed.
Build-Along Steps
01 · Gather the SetCollect the three studies, final artwork, palette notes, and Wednesday technique evidence.
02 · Arrange the ComparisonPlace the three studies together so differences can be compared quickly.
03 · Add Variable LabelsIdentify hue, value, and saturation clearly.
04 · Add Final-Choice RationaleState which study was developed and why.
05 · PreviewCheck color reproduction, labels, readability, and whether the comparison remains understandable.
06 · Export & Record LocationSave the master and export in the Week 5 evidence system.
Real-World Research · Cost/Time · Preview/Test

Research

Review two professional artist or museum artwork records and identify which factual details are consistently documented.

Cost

No paid portfolio service is required; an offline page or PDF fully satisfies the lesson.

Time

Estimate the time needed to assemble the record and compare with actual build time.

Test

A viewer should be able to identify the artwork, date, medium/tool, subject, and why the record matters.

Required Business Evidence
Functional Check · 80% Mastery

Answer all five questions. Four correct answers are required.

1. Why include all three color studies?
2. What should the record identify?
3. What makes the rationale useful?
4. What should happen after export?
5. Is paid portfolio software required?
Complete the business evidence and functional check to evaluate mastery.
