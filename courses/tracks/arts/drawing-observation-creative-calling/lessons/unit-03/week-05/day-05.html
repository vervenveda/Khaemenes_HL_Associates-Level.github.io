About This Marketing Day

Today you present the Week 5 Color Study Process Portfolio Record to one appropriate viewer and test whether the hue, value, saturation comparisons and final color choice are understandable. Public posting is not required; private and offline review fully satisfy the assignment.

Marketing & Promotion Day supports Week 05 · Hue, Value and Saturation. Likes, follows, reach, and virality are never mastery criteria.

Marketing Day Goals

Present the Comparison

Show the three studies and final artwork in a clear sequence.

Test Color Clarity

Find out whether the viewer can distinguish hue, value, and saturation differences.

Ask for Specific Feedback

Use a question about color relationships rather than taste.

Record the Learning

Carry one color or presentation insight into Week 6.

Marketing Goal

Safety Rule

Communication Principle · Choose Channel
Principle · Show the VariablesLet the viewer compare hue, value, saturation, and the final artwork side by side.
Principle · Discuss Color Evidence, Not TasteAsk what changed visually rather than whether the viewer likes a color.
Channel · Private/OfflineIn-person review, printed page, classroom review, or family critique all count fully.
Channel · DigitalEmail, private message, or controlled portfolio review may be used when appropriate.
Feedback RuleAsk one question about which color property changed and whether the final choice is understandable.
Prepare · Preview · Present
01 · Prepare the RecordOpen Thursday’s color-study process page.
02 · Write a One-Sentence IntroductionExplain that the page compares hue, value, and saturation before showing the final artwork.
03 · PreviewCheck color reproduction, labels, dates, readability, privacy, and image clarity.
04 · Choose the ChannelSelect one safe private/offline or appropriate digital review method.
05 · PresentLet the viewer examine the comparison before you explain which study was chosen.
06 · Ask the Color QuestionAsk: “Which study changes hue, which changes value, and which changes saturation—and can you see why the final palette was chosen?”
Response Tracker · Signal vs Vanity Metrics

Useful Signal

The viewer correctly identifies a hue, value, or saturation difference or points out an unclear comparison.

Weak Signal

“I like this color” may be useful preference information but does not prove the comparison is clear.

Vanity Metric

Likes, followers, impressions, and virality do not determine mastery.

Follow-Up

Carry one useful color or presentation insight into Week 6.
