About This Creative Day

Creative Day develops the weekly studio theme: Surface, Relief and Revision. Create a safe low-relief study or drawn relief simulation, observe it under changing light, critique structure and legibility, and document revision.

Adapted from the preserved Bazaar Art ART101 · Week 22 source lesson.

Creative Day Goals

Observe / Research

Begin from direct evidence, reference material, or a documented design problem.

Experiment

Create alternatives before committing to one solution.

Create & Revise

Develop an original work and revise from comparison, critique, or test evidence.

Archive Evidence

Preserve process, final work, reflection, and source information where relevant.

Materials & Accessible Alternative

Primary Tools

Use appropriate drawing, design, photo, print, collage, or digital tools already available.

No-Purchase Path

Ordinary paper, pencil, found materials, drawn simulation, or offline digital equivalents count where appropriate.

Evidence Device

Use a camera/scanner if available; otherwise use written diagrams or drawn records.

Safety / Privacy

Work only in permitted settings and protect personal information, consent, and source rights.

Creative Action Steps
01 · Research / ObserveIdentify the subject, design problem, source evidence, or contextual question for the week.
02 · Make AlternativesCreate at least two exploratory versions, studies, frames, prototypes, or layout options.
03 · DevelopChoose one direction and build a substantial original work.
04 · TestCompare the result against the week’s technical or communication goal.
05 · ReviseMake one documented change based on evidence.
06 · ArchiveSave process, final work, metadata, reflection, and sources.
