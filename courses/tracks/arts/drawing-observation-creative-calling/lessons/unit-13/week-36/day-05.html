Marketing Goal · Audience · Work

Goal

Artist Defense & Portfolio Review

Audience

Teacher, family member, critique partner, classmate, collaborator-style reviewer, or another appropriate viewer.

Work / Offer

Final Portfolio Exhibition & Defense Packet

Safety Rule

Public posting is never required; consent and privacy take priority.

Communication Principle · Choose Channel
01 · Lead with PurposeState what the work is meant to communicate.
02 · Choose ChannelUse in-person, print, email, private message, controlled portfolio, classroom, or public channel only when appropriate.
03 · Protect ContextPreserve sources, consent, uncertainty, and limitations.
04 · Ask One Specific QuestionTest the week’s intended understanding.
05 · Avoid Popularity ScoringLikes, follows, reach, and virality are not mastery evidence.
Prepare · Preview · Present
01 · PrepareOpen Thursday’s finished asset.
02 · PreviewCheck readability, privacy, captions, source notes, and file quality.
03 · PresentLet the viewer encounter the work without coaching.
04 · AskUse a question tied to Artist Defense & Portfolio Review.
05 · RecordCapture the response accurately.
06 · Follow UpChoose one evidence-based improvement.
Response Tracker · Signal vs Vanity Metrics

Useful Signal

Specific evidence about comprehension, hierarchy, usability, context, or a visible problem.

Weak Signal

General praise/dislike often lacks actionable detail.

Vanity Metric

Likes, followers, impressions, and virality do not determine mastery.

Follow-Up

Keep, revise, or test next without automatically obeying every opinion.

Required Marketing Evidence
Promotion Check · 80% Mastery

Answer all five questions. Four correct answers are required.

1. What should guide success in this lesson?
2. Why preserve process evidence?
3. How should claims, sources, or observations be handled?
4. What should revision respond to?
5. What must be archived or recorded?
Complete all required evidence and the mastery check.
