Business Asset of the Day

Asset

Capstone Project Dossier

Purpose

Translate studio learning into a professional record, proposal, presentation, or evidence packet.

Format

Printable page, PDF, image export, slide, or offline equivalent.

Boundary

No purchase, public posting, or popularity result is required.

Requirements & Finished Example
01 · Primary WorkInclude the substantial Week project or selected evidence.
02 · Process EvidenceShow studies, iterations, critique, or revision.
03 · MetadataRecord title, date, medium/tool, course/unit/week, and context.
04 · Sources / PermissionsRecord attribution, consent, citation, or licensing where applicable.
05 · ReflectionExplain one decision, revision, and limitation.
Build-Along Steps
01 · GatherCollect the strongest evidence without deleting process.
02 · OrganizeCreate a clear hierarchy and reading order.
03 · AnnotateAdd metadata and source notes.
04 · PreviewTest readability, completeness, privacy, and file quality.
05 · ReviseCorrect one usability problem.
06 · Export & ArchiveSave master and export; record location.
