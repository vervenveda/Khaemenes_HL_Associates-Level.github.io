Baseline Observation

ARTS-111 · Unit 01 · Lesson 01 · Creative Day

Learner Profile Parent Profile Enrollment

About This Lesson

This first lesson creates an honest starting point. You are not trying to make a beautiful drawing. You are recording how you currently observe, measure, simplify, correct, and make visual decisions so future growth can be compared against real evidence.

Lesson Goals

Observe

Draw from a real object or scene rather than from memory or a symbol.

Document

Create a baseline image that can become the first evidence item in your artist portfolio.

Reflect

Notice your current habits without grading your artistic style, taste, or personality.

Establish Routine

Begin a repeatable studio habit: prepare, work, document, reflect, archive.

Materials

Drawing Surface

Paper, sketchbook, tablet, or another surface you can document clearly.

Drawing Tool

Pencil, pen, charcoal, stylus, or another simple mark-making tool.

Observed Subject

A real object, still life, room corner, plant, tool, shoe, hand, or similar subject.

Evidence Device

Camera, scanner, screenshot tool, or export function for documenting the finished study.

Creative Action Steps
01 · ChooseSelect one real subject that can remain in place for at least twenty minutes.
02 · Look Before DrawingSpend one full minute observing large shapes, angles, proportions, negative spaces, and relationships.
03 · Draw for 20 MinutesWork continuously. Correct when needed, but do not erase the entire drawing to make it look polished.
04 · Stop on TimeAt twenty minutes, stop. The goal is a baseline record, not a finished exhibition piece.
05 · Photograph or ExportCreate one clear image of the work. Keep the whole drawing visible and avoid decorative filters.
06 · ReflectRecord what you noticed about seeing, measuring, correcting, confidence, hesitation, and decision-making.
