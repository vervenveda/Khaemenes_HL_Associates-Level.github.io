About This Marketing Day

Today you present the Week 8 artwork and its information card to a defined audience, then learn from the response. Public posting is optional; a private, classroom, family, critique-partner, or offline presentation fully satisfies the assignment.

Marketing & Promotion Day supports Week 08 · Focal Point and Visual Hierarchy. Likes, follows, reach, and virality are never mastery criteria.

Marketing Day Goals

Define the Audience

Choose who should see the work and why that audience is appropriate.

Prepare the Message

Use the artwork card to communicate clearly without over-explaining.

Ask for Useful Feedback

Use one specific question tied to hierarchy, clarity, or interpretation.

Learn from Response

Record evidence-based observations rather than chasing popularity.

Marketing Goal · Audience · Work

Marketing Goal

Present the artwork clearly and learn whether the focal hierarchy communicates as intended.

Audience

Choose a critique partner, family member, teacher, classmate, portfolio reviewer, or another appropriate viewer.

Work / Offer

The Week 8 artwork plus the finished artwork information card.

Safety Rule

Public posting is not required. Use a private or offline presentation whenever that is safer or more appropriate.

Communication Principle · Choose Channel
Principle · Lead with the WorkLet the artwork and title carry the first impression; keep supporting copy concise.
Principle · Match the AudienceUse language and context appropriate to the viewer rather than trying to appeal to everyone.
Channel · Private/OfflineIn-person critique, printed card, family review, classroom display, or private portfolio review all count.
Channel · DigitalA controlled portfolio page, email, private message, or public post may be used only when appropriate.
Feedback RuleAsk one question that can produce useful information about clarity or viewer attention.
Build Promotional Asset · Preview · Share
01 · Prepare the PresentationPair the artwork with the finished information card or a concise equivalent.
02 · Write the Caption / IntroductionUse a short factual introduction that names the work and its purpose.
03 · PreviewCheck image crop, readability, spelling, metadata, and privacy before sharing.
04 · Choose the ChannelSelect one controlled or public channel appropriate to the audience.
05 · Share / PresentComplete one real audience-facing action, online or offline.
06 · Ask the Feedback QuestionAsk: “Where did your eye go first, and what made it go there?” or another specific hierarchy question.
Response Tracker · Signal vs Vanity Metrics

Useful Signal

Specific viewer comments about focal point, hierarchy, readability, interpretation, or confusion.

Weak Signal

A response such as “nice” may be encouraging but gives little information for revision.

Vanity Metric

Likes, follower counts, impressions, and virality do not determine artistic or course mastery.

Follow-Up

Choose one useful response to consider for future presentation or revision; disagreement does not require automatic change.

Required Marketing Evidence

2. Is public posting required?
3. Which feedback question is more useful?
4. Which is a vanity metric?
