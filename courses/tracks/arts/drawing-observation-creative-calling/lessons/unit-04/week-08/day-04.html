About This Artpreneur Day

Today you turn the Week 8 artwork into a professional presentation asset: an artwork information card. The goal is to present the work clearly with hierarchy, accurate metadata, a concise description, and a usable visual layout.

Artpreneur Day supports Week 08 · Focal Point and Visual Hierarchy.

Artpreneur Day Goals

Build a Real Asset

Create a professional artwork information card rather than a practice-only exercise.

Use Hierarchy

Make the artwork title, image, and essential metadata easy to scan.

Record Accurate Details

Use truthful title, medium, dimensions or format, date, and description.

Test Usability

Check readability at normal screen or print size before archiving the asset.

Business Asset of the Day

Asset

One artwork information card for the Week 8 focal-point artwork.

Purpose

Give a portfolio viewer, teacher, curator, or future client the essential facts without clutter.

Format

Use a printable page, PDF, image export, or offline digital layout.

Privacy Rule

Do not add personal contact information unless an adult learner intentionally chooses to do so.

Requirements & Finished Example
Required · Artwork ImageUse a clear photo, scan, or export of the Week 8 artwork.
Required · TitleGive the work a readable title with clear visual priority.
Required · MetadataInclude date, medium, and dimensions or digital format when applicable.
Required · DescriptionWrite a concise 2–4 sentence description of the visual intention or process.
Hierarchy RuleThe viewer should understand the work first and supporting text second.
Build-Along Steps
01 · Prepare the ArtworkCrop or export the image cleanly without altering the artwork’s meaning.
02 · Enter MetadataRecord title, medium, date, and dimensions or format accurately.
03 · Write the DescriptionKeep the text concise and specific to the work.
04 · Build the LayoutUse the hierarchy skills from Monday and Wednesday to organize image, title, and supporting text.
05 · Preview & TestCheck readability, alignment, spacing, and image quality at normal viewing size.
06 · Export & Record LocationSave the finished card and record where the master file and exported version live.
Real-World Research · Cost/Time · Preview/Test

Research

Review two museum, gallery, or professional portfolio artwork labels/cards and note what information is consistently present.

Cost

The asset must be completable with existing tools; purchasing design software is not required.

Time

Estimate build time before starting, then record actual time afterward.

Test

Ask whether a viewer can identify the artwork title, image, medium, and description quickly.

Required Business Evidence
Functional Check · 80% Mastery

Answer all five questions. Four correct answers are required.

1. What is the primary job of an artwork information card?
2. Which metadata should be accurate?
3. Why test the asset at normal viewing size?
4. What should happen after export?
5. What is the cost rule?
Complete the business evidence and functional check to evaluate mastery.
