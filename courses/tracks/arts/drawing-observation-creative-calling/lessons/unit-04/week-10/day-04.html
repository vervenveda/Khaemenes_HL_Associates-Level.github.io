About This Artpreneur Day

Today you turn the Week 10 visual narrative into a professional project pitch sheet. The asset summarizes the premise, audience, visual approach, sequence sample, research/rights considerations, and deliverables in a form that could support a portfolio review, collaborator conversation, grant draft, or project proposal.

Artpreneur Day supports Week 10 · Visual Narrative and Storytelling.

Artpreneur Day Goals

State the Premise

Explain the story idea in one clear, concise statement.

Define the Audience

Identify who the project is for without relying on popularity metrics.

Show the Visual Plan

Include selected storyboard frames or a sequence sample.

Document Responsibility

Record source, permission, consent, and representation considerations where relevant.

Business Asset of the Day

Asset

A one-page visual story project pitch sheet.

Purpose

Communicate the project clearly to a reviewer, collaborator, teacher, curator, or future client.

Format

Printable page, PDF, slide, or offline digital presentation page.

Safety & Rights

Use original, licensed, public-domain, or appropriately permitted content and protect private information.

Requirements & Finished Example
Required · Project Title & PremiseState what happens or changes in one concise sentence.
Required · Audience & FormatIdentify intended audience and final format or length.
Required · Sequence SampleInclude selected storyboard frames or a representative visual.
Required · Context / Rights NoteRecord attribution, consent, permission, or representational considerations when relevant.
Required · DeliverablesList what must exist when the project is complete.
Build-Along Steps
01 · Distill the PremiseReduce the story to one clear sentence without over-explaining.
02 · Define Audience & FormatState who it serves and how the finished narrative will be experienced.
03 · Select Visual EvidenceChoose storyboard frames or a sequence sample that demonstrates the core idea.
04 · Add Context & RightsRecord sources, permissions, consent, privacy, or cultural-context notes where relevant.
05 · List DeliverablesSpecify final files, sequence length, portfolio evidence, and any review materials.
06 · Preview, Export & RecordTest clarity, export the pitch sheet, and record the master file location.
Real-World Research · Cost/Time · Preview/Test

Research

Review two legitimate project proposals, artist project pages, or visual pitch examples and note how they communicate scope and audience.

Cost

Use available or free tools; no purchase or public submission is required.

Time

Estimate pitch-sheet build time and compare it with actual time.

Test

Ask whether a reader can explain the project, audience, and deliverables after one pass.
