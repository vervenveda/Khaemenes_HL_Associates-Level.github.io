About This Marketing Day

Today you present the Week 10 visual-story pitch sheet to an appropriate audience and test whether the project premise, audience, sequence, and deliverables are understandable after one pass. Public posting is optional; a private or offline pitch fully satisfies the lesson.

Marketing & Promotion Day supports Week 10 · Visual Narrative and Storytelling. Likes, follows, reach, and virality are never mastery criteria.

Marketing Day Goals

Pitch Clearly

Communicate the project premise and audience without over-explaining.

Test Comprehension

Find out what a viewer understands after one pass.

Ask a Useful Question

Use feedback tied to clarity, sequence, audience, or deliverables.

Learn Without Chasing Popularity

Record useful response signals and ignore vanity metrics as mastery criteria.

Marketing Goal · Audience · Work

Marketing Goal

Test whether the pitch sheet explains the project, intended audience, and deliverables clearly.

Audience

Choose a reviewer, collaborator-style partner, teacher, family member, or another appropriate viewer.

Work / Offer

The Week 10 visual story pitch sheet and a selected sequence sample.

Safety Rule

Public submission or posting is never required; controlled and offline presentation count fully.

Communication Principle · Choose Channel
Principle · Lead with the WorkLet the artwork and title carry the first impression; keep supporting copy concise.
Principle · Match the AudienceUse language and context appropriate to the viewer rather than trying to appeal to everyone.
Channel · Private/OfflineIn-person critique, printed card, family review, classroom display, or private portfolio review all count.
Channel · DigitalA controlled portfolio page, email, private message, or public post may be used only when appropriate.
Feedback RuleAsk one question that can produce useful information about clarity or viewer attention.
Build Promotional Asset · Preview · Share
01 · Prepare the PitchUse the finished Thursday pitch sheet and selected story frames.
02 · Write the IntroductionSummarize the project in one or two sentences.
03 · PreviewCheck premise, audience, deliverables, source notes, privacy, and visual clarity.
04 · Choose the ChannelSelect a private/offline or appropriate digital presentation method.
05 · PresentLet the viewer examine the pitch without coaching.
06 · Ask the Clarity QuestionAsk: “What do you think this project is, who is it for, and what would be delivered?”
Response Tracker · Signal vs Vanity Metrics

Useful Signal

A viewer can accurately restate the premise, audience, sequence, or deliverables—or points out where one is unclear.

Weak Signal

General approval does not establish that the project brief is understandable.

Vanity Metric

Likes, impressions, shares, and follower counts do not prove project clarity or mastery.

Follow-Up

Choose one clarity issue to revise or one successful communication choice to preserve.
