About This Artpreneur Day

Today you turn the Week 9 composition into a professional portfolio presentation spread. The asset must use balance, movement, spacing, and hierarchy to present the artwork, process evidence, and concise supporting information without visual clutter.

Artpreneur Day supports Week 09 · Balance and Movement.

Artpreneur Day Goals

Build a Portfolio Spread

Create one usable presentation page or two-page equivalent.

Use Balance

Distribute image, text, and empty space intentionally.

Show Process

Include selected study or revision evidence without overwhelming the final work.

Test Readability

Check flow, spacing, image scale, and text hierarchy at normal size.

Business Asset of the Day

Asset

A portfolio presentation spread for the Week 9 artwork.

Purpose

Show final work and selected process evidence in a professional, readable format.

Format

Printable page, PDF, slide, or offline digital portfolio layout.

Privacy Rule

Do not include personal contact details unless intentionally appropriate for an adult learner.

Requirements & Finished Example
Required · Final ArtworkGive the finished composition the strongest visual priority.
Required · Process EvidenceInclude one or two thumbnails, balance studies, or revision images.
Required · MetadataInclude title, medium, date, and dimensions or format.
Required · ReflectionAdd a short note explaining one balance/movement decision.
Layout RuleUse whitespace, scale, and alignment so process supports rather than competes with the final artwork.
Build-Along Steps
01 · Select EvidenceChoose the final artwork and only the process images that best explain a meaningful decision.
02 · Establish the GridSet margins, columns, alignment, and a clear visual axis.
03 · Place the Final WorkGive it dominant scale and enough surrounding space.
04 · Add ProcessPlace secondary images so they create rhythm without competing with the final work.
05 · Add TextKeep metadata and reflection concise and aligned to the layout system.
06 · Preview, Export & RecordTest the spread at normal size, export it, and record the file location.
Real-World Research · Cost/Time · Preview/Test

Research

Review two artist portfolios, exhibition catalogs, or institutional portfolio pages and note image-to-text balance.

Cost

Use existing or free tools; no paid layout software is required.

Time

Estimate layout time and compare it with actual build time.

Test

Ask whether the eye reaches the final artwork first and the process evidence second.

Required Business Evidence
Functional Check · 80% Mastery

Answer all five questions. Four correct answers are required.

1. What should receive the strongest emphasis in this portfolio spread?
2. Why include process evidence?
3. What role does whitespace play?
4. What is a useful preview test?
5. What must happen after export?
Complete the business evidence and functional check to evaluate mastery.
