About This CEO Day

Today you manage the balance-and-movement project as a production job. You will choose an active version, allocate time and materials, schedule critique and revision, and preserve version evidence so decisions remain traceable.

CEO Day supports Week 09 · Balance and Movement without grading artistic taste.

CEO Day Goals

Choose a Version

Select the strongest exploratory composition using observable criteria.

Allocate Time

Budget studio time between production, critique, revision, and documentation.

Protect Evidence

Keep versions instead of overwriting the process.

Plan Revision

Schedule one specific critique and revision checkpoint.
