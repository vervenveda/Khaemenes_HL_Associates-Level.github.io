About This Skills Day

Today you deepen value technique by isolating light direction, value separation, core shadow, reflected light, cast shadow, and edge transitions. You will practice those relationships separately before transferring one tonal correction into Monday’s form study.

Skills & Technique Day supports Week 04 · Value and Form.

Skills & Technique Goals

Separate Values

Keep neighboring light and shadow families distinct enough to read.

Read Light Logic

Connect highlight, form shadow, reflected light, and cast shadow to one light source.

Control Edges

Use harder and softer transitions where the observed form requires them.

Transfer the Skill

Apply one evidence-based tonal correction to Monday’s study.

Skill of the Day

Visual Target

Create a simple form study whose light direction and shadow families read coherently.

Prerequisite Check

Review Monday’s study and identify the tonal relationship that least supports volume.

Concept

Volume emerges from organized value relationships, not from blending everything smoothly.

Common Mistake

Making reflected light brighter than the light-facing family can break the light logic.

Segmented Demonstration & Key Cues
01 · Identify Light DirectionState where the strongest light originates before shading.
02 · Group the Light FamilyKeep light-facing areas related and distinct from the shadow family.
03 · Establish Core/Form ShadowDarken the turning-away area enough to describe volume.
04 · Place Reflected LightKeep reflected light subordinate to the main light family.
05 · Check Cast ShadowMake sure its direction agrees with the light source and contact with the surface.
Practice Sequence
Warm-UpCreate a fresh five-step grayscale with clear separation between each value.
Controlled ExerciseShade a simple sphere/box form using only five grouped values.
Guided PracticeAdd core shadow, reflected light, and cast shadow while preserving the light direction.
Self-ComparisonCompare the new study with Monday’s and identify which tonal relationship improved.
RetryIf the form reads flat, strengthen one value separation rather than blending everything.
TransferApply one successful tonal correction to the Week 4 evidence set.
