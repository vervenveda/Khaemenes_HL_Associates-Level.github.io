About This CEO Day

Today you manage Monday’s initial and revised still-life studies as a measured comparison set. You will preserve the before/after evidence, organize the recorded ratios and negative-space checks, choose one proportional issue for Wednesday practice, and keep the project focused enough to finish cleanly.
