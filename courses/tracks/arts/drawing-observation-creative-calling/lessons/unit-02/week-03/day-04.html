About This Artpreneur Day

Today you turn the Week 3 initial/revised still-life work into a professional process record that shows the reference unit, key ratios, negative-space evidence, largest correction, and Wednesday technique transfer.

Artpreneur Day supports Week 03 · Proportion and Relationships.

Artpreneur Day Goals

Show Before & After

Present the initial and revised drawing together.

Document the Method

Include reference unit, selected ratios, and negative-space checks.

Explain the Correction

Connect the largest visible change to the evidence that justified it.

Archive for Growth

Keep the master page and source images organized for later comparison.

Business Asset of the Day

Asset

One Proportion Revision Portfolio Record for the Week 3 still life.

Purpose

Show how comparative measurement led to a visible revision.

Format

Printable page, PDF, image export, or offline digital portfolio page.

Integrity Rule

Preserve the initial drawing; the before/after difference is part of the evidence.

Requirements & Finished Example
Required · Initial StudyInclude the quick first sketch before measurement.
Required · Revised DrawingInclude the drawing created after comparative checks.
Required · Measurement EvidenceShow the reference unit and selected ratios or relationships.
Required · Negative-Space EvidenceInclude at least one clear note or diagram showing how space revealed an error.
Required · Correction NoteExplain the largest proportional change and why it was made.
Build-Along Steps
01 · Gather EvidenceCollect initial drawing, revised drawing, ratio notes, negative-space checks, and Wednesday technique evidence.
02 · Establish Before/After OrderPlace the initial and revised work so the change can be compared immediately.
03 · Add Measurement NotesInclude the reference unit and only the most informative ratios.
04 · Add the Correction ExplanationState what changed and what visible comparison justified it.
05 · PreviewCheck labels, alignment, readability, and whether the correction story is understandable.
06 · Export & Record LocationSave the master and export in the Week 3 evidence system.
Real-World Research · Cost/Time · Preview/Test

Research

Review two professional artist or museum artwork records and identify which factual details are consistently documented.

Cost

No paid portfolio service is required; an offline page or PDF fully satisfies the lesson.

Time

Estimate the time needed to assemble the record and compare with actual build time.

Test

A viewer should be able to identify the artwork, date, medium/tool, subject, and why the record matters.

Required Business Evidence
Functional Check · 80% Mastery

Answer all five questions. Four correct answers are required.

1. Why show both initial and revised drawings?
2. What measurement information is most useful?
3. Why include negative-space evidence?
4. What should the correction note explain?
5. Is paid portfolio software required?
Complete the business evidence and functional check to evaluate mastery.
