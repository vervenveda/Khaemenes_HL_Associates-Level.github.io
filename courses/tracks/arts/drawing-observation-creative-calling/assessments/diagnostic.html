Baseline Artist Inventory

This establishes a starting point, not an aesthetic judgment.

  1. Complete a 20-minute observation drawing from a real object or scene.
  2. Describe current drawing/studio experience and media familiarity.
  3. Identify three strengths and three skills to strengthen.
  4. Record studio setup, tools and safety/access constraints.
  5. Explain observation versus drawing from assumption or symbol.
  6. Attach baseline evidence with title, date, medium and process note.
