Two-Year Integrated Arts Roadmap

Year 1 · Weeks 1–36

ARTS-111 · Foundation to Professional Practice

Observation, drawing, color, composition, figure, perspective, materials, print, photography, design, curation, portfolio evidence and embedded artist-business practice.

Open Year 1

Year 2 · Weeks 37–72

ARTS-211 · Specialization Studio II

Advanced drawing and painting, cohesive series development, exhibition readiness, professional documentation, contracts/rights literacy and independent capstone practice.

Open Year 2

Every Week

Five-Day Learning Rhythm

Monday Creative Day · Tuesday CEO Day · Wednesday Skills & Technique · Thursday Artpreneur Day · Friday Marketing & Promotion.

Embedded Across Both Years

Professional Practice

Specialization Studio · Artist Business · Marketing/Brand/Audience · Exhibition/Clients/Galleries · Call for Art Registry · Firmament Law documents when relevant.

Assessment & Retention Spine

Weekly

Lesson Mastery

Required lesson evidence, professional-practice completion, vocabulary retrieval and objective knowledge checks use an 80% mastery floor. Missing required evidence cannot be averaged away.

Weeks 18 & 36 of Each Year

Midterm & Final

Year midterms are open-book cumulative assessments. Year finals are closed-book cumulative assessments. Both require 80% mastery with correction and retry.

Weeks 9 · 18 · 27 · 36

Portfolio Checkpoints

Students curate studio work, revision evidence, professional-practice artifacts, growth reflection and limitations so development remains visible across the full two years.

Year 2 · Week 36

Artist Defense

The final capstone integrates the professional series, portfolio, business records, audience communication, legal/rights awareness, exhibition readiness and a 12-month post-certificate plan.

Two-Year Integrated Capstone

The certificate ends with a cumulative Artist Defense rather than a separate third-year course. Students defend decisions using direct evidence, show technical and professional growth, identify limitations honestly, and present a practical 12-month continuation plan.

Mastery is never based on taste, sales, gallery acceptance, prestige, follower count or virality.
