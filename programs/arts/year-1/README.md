# Arts Year 1 · Discover, Build Skill, Find Direction

Year 1 is the practical foundation of the Associate-Level Certificate in Arts, Studio Practice & Creative Enterprise.

## Structure

- 36 weeks
- 5 concentration courses
- 15 concentration learning units
- 13 curriculum units per course
- 3 internal learning units per course
- minimum 3 hands-on studio-production days every week
- integrated portfolio, critique, artist writing, business and market evidence throughout the year

The authoritative year-level map is `YEAR-1-BLUEPRINT.json`. Individual course maps live under `courses/tracks/arts/`.

## Year 1 courses

1. ARTS-111 · Drawing, Observation & Creative Calling
2. ARTS-121 · 2D Design, Color & Visual Communication
3. ARTS-131 · 3D, Material, Space & Time-Based Production Lab
4. ARTS-141 · Artist Research, Critique, Portfolio & Professional Writing
5. ARTS-151 · Creative Enterprise, Niche & Market Discovery

## Exit condition

The learner enters Year 2 with a curated portfolio, documented studio practice, artist bio/statements, niche hypothesis, basic pricing/cost model, small launch evidence and a written specialization direction.
