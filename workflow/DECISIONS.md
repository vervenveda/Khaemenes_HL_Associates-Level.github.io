# Decision Register

This file records durable program decisions. Add new entries; do not silently rewrite history.

## D-001 · Dedicated Associate-Level repository

**Decision:** The two-year Associate-Level Certificate owns its own repository.

**Reason:** Higher Learning had become too broad for clean course ownership and future growth. A dedicated repository gives the two-year program one canonical home and allows later bachelor-level programs to remain separate.

## D-002 · Credential language

**Decision:** Use **Associate-Level Certificate**, not Associate Degree.

**Boundary:** Free and non-accredited. Internal learning units are not institutional college credits. No guaranteed transfer, admission, employment, licensure, representation, sales or income.

## D-003 · 60-unit model

**Decision:** 60 internal learning units = 24 shared core + 30 concentration + 6 capstone.

**Guidance:** ~15 documented learning hours per internal unit.

## D-004 · Five initial tracks

Arts, Sciences, Civics, Health, Business.

**Reason:** The ecosystem already contains meaningful source material for all five.

## D-005 · Shared-source migration

**Decision:** Migrate reusable halls with provenance; do not automatically treat them as courses.

**Reason:** One source hall can support several programs without creating competing copies.

## D-006 · Full-course migration

**Decision:** Do not duplicate full courses merely for convenience.

**Rule:** Ownership transfers only when this repository is intended to become canonical; validate first, then retire/redirect the former owner.

## D-007 · Shared core standard

**Decision:** Eight common courses, each 3 internal units / ~45 hours / 12 modules / 36 lessons.

**Result:** 24 units / ~360 hours / 288 lessons.

## D-008 · Arts identity

**Decision:** Arts education is centered on becoming a working artist.

**Reason:** Practical production, portfolio, business, market and audience skills have higher value for this program than recreating a prestige-school experience.

## D-009 · Arts weekly rhythm

**Decision:** Five-day concentration week with a minimum of three hands-on studio-production days.

- Mon — Studio
- Tue — Critique / Portfolio / Artist Writing
- Wed — Studio
- Thu — Business / Marketing / Career
- Fri — Studio / Finish / Document / Archive

## D-010 · Arts course standard

**Decision:** Each Arts concentration course is 36 weeks and 13 curriculum units.

## D-011 · What to borrow from leading art schools

**Keep:** drawing/seeing, 2D design, 3D space/material, 4D/time, research, critique, visual culture, collaboration, sustainability, exhibition/audience, broad foundation before specialization.

**Reframe/reject:** prestige signaling, unnecessary material cost, jargon-driven critique, theory detached from making, portfolio polish without business literacy, single-canon gatekeeping.

## D-012 · Arts specialization timing

**Decision:** Broad Year 1, specialization Year 2.

**Reason:** Students should test media, subject, style and working methods before committing to a signature practice and niche.

## D-013 · Portfolio from day one

**Decision:** Portfolio building is continuous.

Every candidate portfolio work should be documented, titled/identified, dated, measured where relevant, photographed/scanned, and entered in the learner inventory.

## D-014 · Business from year one

**Decision:** Career/business education begins before specialization.

Year 1 includes niche discovery, audience, pricing logic, costs, market research, branding, outreach and a small launch experiment.

## D-015 · Arts capstone

**Decision:** Capstone is a professional launch, not only an exhibition.

Required evidence covers body of work + statements + bio/CV + inventory + pricing + business operations + marketing campaign + professional presentation + outreach + public launch/presentation + defense.
