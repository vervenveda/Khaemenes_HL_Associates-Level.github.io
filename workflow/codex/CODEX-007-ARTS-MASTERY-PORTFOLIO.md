# Conversation Codex 007 · Arts Mastery, Completion & Portfolio Evidence

## Decision

The Arts program uses a **completion-and-mastery grading system**, not a subjective taste grade.

A lesson or assignment is complete only when:

1. every required input section is completed
2. every scored knowledge/mastery section is at least 80%
3. required vocabulary work is at least 80%
4. the relevant weekly quiz/checkpoint is at least 80%
5. every required visual/business artifact has image/screenshot/photo evidence uploaded to the portfolio
6. required metadata/reflection is complete

The student cannot average around a missing section.

## Portfolio evidence rule

For visual and business assignments, completion credit requires portfolio evidence.

Examples:
- artwork
- drawing/painting/3D studies
- business cards
- website screenshots
- portfolio screenshots
- social posts/promotional graphics
- event display/signage
- marketing assets
- artist-call visual materials

The portfolio therefore becomes both:
- a professional body of work
- the evidence ledger used to verify assignment completion

## Knowledge structure

Each unit/course will include:
- vocabulary module
- weekly unit quizzes
- midterm
- final exam

Mastery floor: **80%**.

Below 80%:
- student reviews/corrects
- retries
- existing work/evidence is not discarded
- next gated lesson remains locked until mastery

## Artistic freedom boundary

The course does not grade whether art is beautiful, trendy, prestigious or stylistically approved.

It can grade:
- was the assigned problem completed?
- were required techniques/constraints attempted?
- was required evidence uploaded?
- were required fields/documentation completed?
- did the learner demonstrate knowledge at >=80%?
- does a business asset function according to its checklist?
