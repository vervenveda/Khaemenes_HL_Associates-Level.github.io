# Arts Unit + Week Mentor Layer

Version 2 of the shared Arts guidance contract adds two contextual mentoring levels.

## Unit Mentor Notes
Each of the 13 units in both Year 1 and Year 2 has:
- the unit's core mentoring objective;
- three diagnostic checks;
- critique sentence stems;
- a concrete weak-vs-strong evidence example.

## This Week's Studio Clinic
All 72 weeks receive:
- the week title/focus;
- an establish/develop/integrate studio phase;
- one diagnostic question;
- one troubleshooting cue;
- one critique sentence stem;
- a concrete evidence example.

The clinic complements the five day-role coaching blocks. It does not change scoring or introduce subjective artistic grading.
