# Arts Guidance Layer · Shared Lesson Contract

The Arts program uses one shared guidance engine across active Year 1 and Year 2 lesson pages.

## Placement

The engine inserts **Guidance · How to Work Through This Lesson** immediately before the lesson evidence accordion. It does not replace authored lesson content.

## Guidance blocks

Every lesson receives:
- a clear first move;
- what to notice while working;
- common traps to avoid;
- a self-check before mastery;
- evidence-specific reminders when labels are available;
- an explicit description of what mastery means.

Year 2 also receives an independence/decision-defense block.

## Guardrails

Guidance never:
- scores artistic taste, beauty, style, popularity or commercial success;
- requires paid tools, public posting, sales or paid opportunity applications;
- substitutes for the lesson’s objective mastery gate;
- changes learner evidence or assessment scores.

The engine derives the day role and lesson title from the active page, so the coaching remains aligned with Creative Day, CEO Day, Skills & Technique, Artpreneur Day, or Marketing & Promotion Day.
