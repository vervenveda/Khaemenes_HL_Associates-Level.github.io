# CODEX-010 · ARTS-111 Monday Creative Day Curriculum

ARTS-111 now has a complete 36-week Monday Creative Day authority derived from the imported Bazaar Art ART101 curriculum.

## Monday rule
Every Monday introduces one major weekly artistic theme through:
1. inspiration,
2. one foundational art skill,
3. one focused studio project,
4. required evidence,
5. reflection,
6. objective knowledge mastery at 80% or higher,
7. portfolio-candidate review.

Artistic taste, beauty, popularity and personal style are never objective mastery criteria.
