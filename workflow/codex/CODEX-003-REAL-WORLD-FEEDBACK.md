# Conversation Codex 003 · Real-World Feedback as Part of the Arts Curriculum

## Problem

The Arts program should not depend on Khaemenes instructors or software to tell a learner whether the artwork is good.

The goal is to teach the learner how working artists actually gather information from the world.

## Decision

Replace the default internal-feedback model with a **real-world feedback practice**.

Khaemenes may teach critique structure, reflection, documentation and evidence review, but it is not the final artistic taste authority.

Students should deliberately put work in front of real people and environments and learn from what happens.

## Feedback channels to integrate

- social media with specific feedback questions
- self-hosted artist critique circles
- studio meetups
- local art groups
- libraries/community centers
- public events
- markets/fairs/festivals
- open studios/pop-up exhibitions
- portfolio reviews with practicing artists
- online art communities
- email/newsletter polls
- portfolio-site feedback forms
- small customer/collector conversations
- peer exchanges and artist swaps
- public work-in-progress sessions

## What students learn

The skill is not “get people to like your art.”

The skill is:

1. decide what you want feedback on
2. choose the right audience
3. ask a useful question
4. record the context
5. look for patterns
6. separate preference from craft/clarity/market feedback
7. ignore harassment/noise
8. decide whether to revise
9. document the decision
10. test again if needed

## Important guardrails

- likes and follower counts are not artistic grades
- virality is not mastery
- one comment does not equal audience consensus
- do not revise every time someone dislikes something
- privacy and safety come before exposure
- pseudonyms and offline/controlled audiences are acceptable
- minors require parent/guardian/Academy safety rules for public posting
- abusive or bad-faith comments are not critique
- feedback should be anonymized in stored course evidence where possible

## Curriculum integration

ARTS-141 now teaches external feedback question design, critique framing, response synthesis and revision/no-revision rationale.

ARTS-151 now uses real audience conversations, public response and launch experiments as market research.

Year 1 requires at least three documented real-world feedback activities using different channels.
