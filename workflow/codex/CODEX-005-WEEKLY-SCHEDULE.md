# Conversation Codex 005 · Canonical Arts Weekly Schedule

## Decision

The Arts program now uses a five-role weekly operating schedule designed around the reality of a working artist.

## Monday · Creative Day

Purpose: protect the artist's creativity from being consumed by business administration.

Students make original work, experiment, sketch, develop ideas, continue personal projects, or push a series forward.

Required evidence: meaningful creative work or documented work-in-progress.

## Tuesday · CEO Day

Purpose: teach the learner to manage the artist's career as a business.

Activities include:
- weekly planning
- calendar and deadlines
- inventory
- expenses and revenue
- opportunity pipeline
- application/admin work
- client follow-up
- records
- Artpreneurship Growth Fund review

Required evidence: updated management record/dashboard/action list.

## Wednesday · Skills & Technique Day

Purpose: deliberate craft growth.

Activities include:
- drawing and observation
- composition/color studies
- material technique
- digital skills
- 3D/4D technique
- technical exercises
- attributed master studies
- problem-solving/revision

Required evidence: skill study, technical exercise or revised work showing deliberate practice.

## Thursday · Artpreneur Day

Purpose: build the actual infrastructure and revenue pathways of the artist's career.

Activities include:
- website
- online portfolio
- pricing
- business cards
- sales systems
- opportunity research
- artist calls
- exhibit/vending preparation
- invoices/contracts basics
- reinvestment planning

Required evidence: usable business asset or concrete opportunity action.

## Friday · Marketing & Promotion Day

Purpose: put the work into the world and develop an audience.

Activities include:
- photographing/scanning work
- portfolio updates
- social/media content
- email/newsletter
- public feedback questions
- event promotion
- networking/follow-up
- press kit
- audience-response review

Required evidence: public-facing promotional asset, outreach action or documented feedback activity.

## Governing principle

The learner practices five roles every week:

**Artist → CEO → Craftsperson → Artpreneur → Promoter**

This replaces the earlier generic three-studio-day model.

The program still protects making:
- Monday is dedicated creative production.
- Wednesday is dedicated technical/skills development.
- Additional studio time can happen whenever a project requires it.

The shift is intentional: a professional artist must learn not only to make work but to manage, improve, monetize and communicate the practice.
