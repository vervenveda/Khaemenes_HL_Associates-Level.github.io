# Conversation Codex 001 · Associate-Level Arts Program

**Codex purpose:** Preserve the high-value decisions from the design conversation that established the practical two-year Arts concentration.

## Starting question

The project began by researching what the first two years of highly regarded global undergraduate art programs commonly teach.

The useful shared pattern identified was:
- drawing/observation
- 2D composition/design
- 3D material/space
- 4D/time-based practice
- art history/visual culture
- writing/research
- critique
- broad experimentation before specialization
- collaboration/sustainability
- exhibition/audience/public presentation

## Founder correction / governing insight

The program should **not** become an imitation of expensive prestige art schools.

The governing priority is practicality:

> Students should find their calling early, build a portfolio immediately, learn to write artist statements, identify a niche, create a marketing strategy, learn the business side of an art career, and spend at least three days of a five-day week physically making art.

This shifted the curriculum from “foundation-school imitation” to a **working-artist professional-practice model**.

## Synthesized decision

Take the best educational mechanics from leading programs but make each one serve a working-artist outcome.

Examples:

- Drawing foundation → stronger observation, decision-making and portfolio work
- 2D design → stronger composition and market/presentation readability
- 3D/4D foundation → discovery of media and niche beyond flat work
- Research studio → stronger source references and original work
- Critique → revision and professional feedback tolerance
- Art history/visual culture → context and informed creative choices
- Collaboration → communication and shared-production skills
- Sustainability → studio safety, materials, waste and cost decisions
- Exhibition/audience → professional placement, documentation and audience testing
- Specialization → cohesive signature body of work and market position

## Weekly architecture

Three studio-production days are protected across the whole Arts concentration:

- Monday — make
- Tuesday — critique / portfolio / writing
- Wednesday — make
- Thursday — business / marketing / career
- Friday — make / finish / document / archive

This is a program-wide weekly spine, not a claim that each simultaneous course independently consumes three full studio days.

## Two-year architecture

### Year 1 · Discover, Build Skill, Find Direction

ARTS-111 Drawing, Observation & Creative Calling  
ARTS-121 2D Design, Color & Visual Communication  
ARTS-131 3D, Material, Space & Time-Based Production Lab  
ARTS-141 Artist Research, Critique, Portfolio & Professional Writing  
ARTS-151 Creative Enterprise, Niche & Market Discovery

### Year 2 · Specialize, Produce, Market, Launch

ARTS-211 Specialization Studio I · 2D / 3D / 4D Signature Practice  
ARTS-221 Specialization Studio II · Cohesive Body of Work & Audience  
ARTS-231 Artist Business · Pricing, Sales, Contracts & Records  
ARTS-241 Marketing, Brand, Audience & Digital Presence  
ARTS-251 Exhibition, Clients, Galleries & Professional Practice

ARTS-298 Professional Artist Launch · Portfolio, Exhibition & Business Capstone

## Course standard

Each concentration course:
- 36 weeks
- 13 curriculum units
- 3 internal learning units

Program:
- 30 concentration units
- 6 capstone units
- plus 24 shared collegiate core units

## What makes this Khaemenes-specific

The program does not treat artistic seriousness as dependent on:
- tuition price
- institutional prestige
- expensive studio materials
- jargon-heavy critique
- adherence to one canon
- gallery acceptance

The program does treat artistic seriousness as dependent on:
- consistent making
- observation and technical growth
- experimentation
- revision
- coherent bodies of work
- documentation
- language for explaining the work
- market awareness
- pricing and cost knowledge
- contracts and records
- audience development
- professional presentation
- resilience and continued practice
