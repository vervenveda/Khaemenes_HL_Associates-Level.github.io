# Conversation Codex 006 · Five Arts Lesson Engines

## Question

What online-learning and page structures are most effective for artistic online education, and how should those findings shape the five-day Khaemenes Arts schedule?

## Research synthesis

The evidence favors:
- segmented, learner-controlled instruction
- removing extraneous page content
- signaling essential information
- visual demonstrations
- authentic projects
- clear weekly structure and next actions
- practice immediately after instruction
- evidence capture
- reflective revision
- accessibility
- meaningful audience/community interaction

Art programs commonly combine lectures, demos, assignments, practice and critique. Khaemenes keeps those strengths but routes feedback toward real audiences rather than treating the school as an artistic taste authority.

## Decision

Build **five separate lesson engines**, not one generic lesson shell.

1. Monday — Creative Day Engine
2. Tuesday — CEO Day Engine
3. Wednesday — Skills & Technique Day Engine
4. Thursday — Artpreneur Day Engine
5. Friday — Marketing & Promotion Day Engine

## Central UX principle

Every page answers four things immediately:

1. What role am I practicing today?
2. What am I doing?
3. What will I produce?
4. What do I do first?

There is only one dominant CTA at a time.

## Why five engines

Creative flow, management, deliberate technical practice, business-asset construction and marketing/outreach impose different cognitive demands. A single dense template would either overload Creative Day or underserve CEO/Artpreneur work.
