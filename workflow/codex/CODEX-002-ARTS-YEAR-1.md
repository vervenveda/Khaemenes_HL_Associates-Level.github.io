# Conversation Codex 002 · Arts Year 1 Full Course Structure

## Problem

Convert the approved working-artist Arts philosophy into a complete first-year operating curriculum.

## Governing constraints

- 36 weeks
- 5 concentration courses
- 13 curriculum units per course
- 3 internal learning units per course
- minimum 3 hands-on studio-production days every week
- continuous portfolio evidence
- critique and artist writing integrated throughout
- business/marketing/career practice every week
- broad Year 1 exploration should lead to informed Year 2 specialization

## Weekly spine

- Monday — Studio Production I
- Tuesday — Critique · Portfolio · Artist Writing
- Wednesday — Studio Production II
- Thursday — Art Business · Marketing · Career Practice
- Friday — Studio Production III · Finish · Photograph · Archive

## Week distribution decision

Each course uses the same 36-week unit pattern:

3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 36 weeks.

This preserves depth in early/middle foundations while giving the final three synthesis units dedicated time without forcing a rushed single-week finish.

## Year 1 courses

### ARTS-111 · Drawing, Observation & Creative Calling

Purpose: observation, drawing fluency, visual decision-making and early artistic self-knowledge.

Thirteen units:
1. Baseline Skills, Studio Habits & Artist Inventory
2. Seeing, Observation & Visual Decision-Making
3. Gesture, Line & Drawing as Inquiry
4. Shape, Value & Proportion
5. Perspective, Space & Structure
6. Figure, Object & Environment
7. Abstraction from Observation
8. Drawing Across Materials
9. Drawing from Memory & Imagination
10. Critique: Intention, Process & Result
11. Influence Mapping & Visual References
12. Calling, Strengths & Niche Discovery
13. First Cohesive Drawing / Observation Portfolio

### ARTS-121 · 2D Design, Color & Visual Communication

Purpose: practical composition, color, image hierarchy and visual communication across fine-art, print, digital and product contexts.

Thirteen units:
1. Elements & Principles in Practical Use
2. Composition That Directs Attention
3. Color Systems for Working Artists
4. Value, Contrast & Readability
5. Line, Shape, Edge & Texture
6. Typography & Image Relationships
7. Pattern, Rhythm & Repetition
8. Photography, Cropping & Image Selection
9. Narrative Composition & Visual Story
10. Digital Imaging & Hybrid 2D Practice
11. Designing for Print, Screen & Product
12. Critique-Led Revision
13. Professional 2D Portfolio & Style Analysis

### ARTS-131 · 3D, Material, Space & Time-Based Production Lab

Purpose: broad exposure to spatial, material and time-based work before specialization.

Thirteen units:
1. Tool Literacy, Studio Setup & Material Safety
2. Form, Volume, Structure & Balance
3. Construction, Assemblage & Object-Making
4. Surface, Texture & Material Transformation
5. Scale, Site & Installation
6. Movement, Kinetics & Temporary Work
7. Sound as Artistic Material
8. Sequence, Time & Performance
9. Video / Motion / Animation Foundations
10. Photography & Documentation of 3D/4D Work
11. Sustainable Materials, Reuse & Responsible Production
12. Critique: Space, Time, Audience & Experience
13. 3D / 4D Experimental Portfolio

### ARTS-141 · Artist Research, Critique, Portfolio & Professional Writing

Purpose: research, critique, portfolio curation and professional artist language.

Thirteen units:
1. What a Portfolio Is For
2. Artist Research: Museums, Galleries, Archives & Living Artists
3. Visual Culture, Art Histories & Context
4. Research Notebook / Process Journal
5. Critique Methods: Describe, Interpret, Question, Revise
6. Selecting Strong Work
7. Sequencing & Visual Rhythm
8. Artist Bio
9. Short Artist Statement
10. Long Artist Statement
11. Artwork Titles, Captions, Metadata & Provenance
12. Applications, Proposals & Professional Correspondence
13. Year-One Portfolio Review & Defense

### ARTS-151 · Creative Enterprise, Niche & Market Discovery

Purpose: artist career models, niche discovery, audience, cost/pricing logic and first market-facing experiment.

Thirteen units:
1. Artist Career Models & Income Streams
2. Skills, Interests & Market Fit
3. Finding a Niche Without Trapping Yourself
4. Audience & Collector Personas
5. Originals, Prints, Products, Licensing & Services
6. Basic Pricing Logic
7. Cost of Materials, Time & Overhead
8. Market Research & Competitive Positioning
9. Brand Foundations & Visual Identity
10. Basic Social / Portfolio Presence
11. Networking, Outreach & Relationship Building
12. Small Launch Experiment
13. Year-One Career Direction Plan

## Integrated Year 1 milestones

- Week 1 — Baseline Artist Inventory
- Week 9 — Foundation Review I
- Week 18 — Midyear Portfolio + Business Check
- Week 27 — Calling / Niche Review
- Week 34 — Small Launch Experiment
- Week 36 — Year-One Portfolio Defense & Year-Two Direction

## Year 1 exit evidence

By Week 36, a student should have:

- substantial original 2D, 3D and/or time-based work
- foundation evidence in drawing, composition, color, material, spatial and documentation skills
- process/research journal
- critique and revision records
- curated Year 1 portfolio
- professional artwork documentation and metadata
- artist bio
- short artist statement
- long artist statement
- niche hypothesis
- primary/secondary medium decision
- basic pricing and cost model
- audience/market hypothesis
- one small launch experiment
- written Year 2 specialization direction
