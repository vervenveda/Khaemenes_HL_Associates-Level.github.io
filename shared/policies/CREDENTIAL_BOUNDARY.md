# Credential Boundary

The Khaemenes Higher Learning Associate-Level Certificate is a **free, non-accredited educational certificate** designed to organize approximately two years of structured postsecondary study and preparation for later bachelor-level learning.

It is **not** an accredited associate degree. Khaemenes learning units are internal workload/evidence measures, not institutional college credits. Completion does not guarantee transfer, admission, employment, licensure, or acceptance by another institution.

The certificate requires the published 60-unit program, required mastery gates, practical/lab/studio evidence where applicable, corrections, portfolio evidence, and an integrative capstone.

No page, course, transcript, badge, or certificate may describe this credential as accredited or as institutional college credit.
