# Evidence and Mastery Standard

- Required knowledge gates use an **80% mastery floor**.
- Scores below 80% require correction/review rather than silent advancement.
- Laboratory, studio, practical, civic, business, health/safety and performance outcomes require discipline-appropriate evidence.
- Browser activity alone is not formal completion evidence.
- Portfolio artifacts must be attributable to the learner.
- Capstones require an evaluator-facing evidence packet and reflection/oral defense or equivalent review.
- Health materials are educational only and confer no diagnostic, treatment, prescribing, counseling-clinical, or professional practice scope.
- External source halls remain references until converted into certificate-owned course modules with assessments and evidence rules.
